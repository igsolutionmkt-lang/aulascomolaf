The method

Start in English. End up speaking German.

You don't need to pretend you understand everything. In the first lessons, English is your support. As you gain confidence, German takes up more and more space, until the whole lesson is in German.

The teacher

I'm German, and I teach in English or Portuguese.

That lets me explain what usually blocks beginners, without turning the lesson into constant translation.

I don't teach loose phrases to memorise. We work on the language inside the situations where you'll need it: the interview, the landlord, the doctor, your first day at work.

Before you book

Do I need to know anything already?

No. You can start from zero; the plan starts where you are.

Both. Online from any country, or in person at Rua da Pedra do Cão, 440, in Vila Nova de Gaia, right next to Porto.

Can I prepare for a job interview?

Yes. The plan focuses on role vocabulary, answers and interview simulations.

It depends on your goal and timeline. We set it in the first lesson.

Yes. Explanations can be in English, so lessons work for any nationality.
